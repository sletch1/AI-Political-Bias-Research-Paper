# Cover letter — structure

Keep to one page. Structure per plan.md §9.2.

1. **What the paper claims, in one sentence.** The reported political lean of large
   language models is substantially a property of the measurement, and we quantify how
   much.

2. **The single number that makes it matter.** Instrument choice accounts for 52% of
   variance in a measured political position; model identity accounts for 13%.

3. **Why it is not merely a negative result.** The lean reproduces; what fails is the
   comparability of magnitudes across studies. We supply the decomposition that restores
   comparability and a reporting standard that preserves it.

4. **Why NMI rather than a domain venue.** The contribution is a measurement method for a
   class of AI audit that is now widely performed and, on this evidence, not yet
   interpretable. It bears on how the field evaluates models, not on one model family.

5. **What is released.** All 2,280 raw administrations, the scoring adapters against
   authoritative sources, and every analysis script.

6. **Honest scope.** Three further measurement strata are specified but not yet estimated;
   the 13% attributable to model identity is therefore an upper bound.

**Do not** claim novelty of model coverage. plan.md §2.1 records that 26-, 43- and
66-model studies now exist; that claim is dead and asserting it invites an easy rejection.
