**Subject:** Presubmission enquiry — an error budget for political-bias measurement in LLMs

Dear Editors,

I am writing to ask whether the following would be of interest as an Article in *Nature
Machine Intelligence*.

A substantial literature reports that large language models hold left-of-centre political
views, measured with standardized human political questionnaires. That literature's
quantitative claims — that one model is more partisan than another, that alignment shifts
a model's politics by some amount — all require that scores be comparable across studies.
We show they are not.

Administering two standardized instruments to 19 models from 11 organizations, 60 times
each (2,280 administrations), we decompose measured political position into variance
components. Instrument and axis choice accounts for 52% of the variance; model identity
accounts for 13%; residual trial-to-trial noise accounts for 35%. Which questionnaire an
auditor happens to select moves a reported score roughly four times more than which model
is being audited. A multi-trait multi-method analysis then shows the two instruments fail
convergent validity outright: constructs they both claim to measure correlate at r = −0.26
across models, against r = +0.52 for distinct constructs measured by the same instrument,
inverting the Campbell–Fiske criterion.

The finding that models lean left survives. What does not survive is the comparability of
the reported magnitudes, and we propose a minimum reporting standard that would restore it.

We are extending this to the three remaining measurement strata identified in the recent
literature — accommodation to the inferred asker, contamination of instruments present in
pretraining data, and forced-choice response framing — under a single factorial, and
validating the residual against linear probes on model activations.

My question is whether NMI would view a measurement-validity result of this kind as
within scope for an Article, given that the contribution is methodological rather than a
new model or system. I am happy to send the current manuscript.

With thanks,
