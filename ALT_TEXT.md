grouped bars with 95 percent interval whiskers for two panels of instruments. For the two original
instruments: model identity that transfers 17 percent (interval 0 to 40), model-by-instrument 28
percent (12 to 41), model-by-axis-within-instrument 21 percent (11 to 34), trial noise 34 percent
