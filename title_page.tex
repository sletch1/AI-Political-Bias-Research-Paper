\section*{Acknowledgments}
Portions of this manuscript's text were drafted and revised with the assistance of a commercial large language model,
and the Python data-collection, scoring, analysis and figure code was written and refactored with its assistance,
between May 2025 and November 2026, used interactively through a paid subscription. It generated no data. The author
reviewed all output, re-derived the reported numbers from the raw data with the released scripts in a clean
environment, directed all content and analysis decisions, and takes full responsibility for the manuscript. Writing
assistance is also stated in the cover letter.

\subsection*{Data availability}
All raw per-trial data, scoring adapters and analysis code are publicly available at
\url{https://github.com/sletch1/AI-Political-Bias-Research-Paper} and archived on Zenodo:
Letchumanan, S. (2026) \emph{An Error Budget for Political-Bias Measurement in Large Language Models: Data and Code}
[dataset]. Zenodo. \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22968157} (concept DOI; resolves to the latest version). A single
script (\texttt{run\_all.sh}) reproduces every number, table and figure offline from the archived raw trials; data
collection cannot be re-run identically because hosted models change. Replication materials (data, syntax, explanatory
memo, log, robustness analyses and a README) will be deposited in the Research \& Politics Dataverse on acceptance.
