This repository contains the code and analysis supporting the research paper “Echoes of Ideology: Evaluating Political Bias and Stability in Large Language Models Through Quantitative Analysis and Standardized Typology Testing,” which empirically examines political bias and ideological consistency in GPT-4, Claude, and DeepSeek. The study evaluates model outputs along two standardized ideological dimensions—economic ideology on a scale from –10 (strongly economically liberal) to +10 (strongly economically conservative), and social ideology on a scale from –10 (strongly libertarian) to +10 (strongly authoritarian)—using repeated trials of the Political Compass and 8Values typology tests. After standardization and data cleaning, the analysis applies descriptive statistics and a formal inferential pipeline consisting of a one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s Honestly Significant Difference (HSD) post-hoc test to determine whether mean ideology scores differ significantly across models and to identify which model pairs differ while controlling for family-wise error. The repository includes separate scripts for economic and social ideology analyses, prints interpretable statistical outputs, and is designed for transparency, reproducibility, and academic rigor, with the full research paper included to provide methodological context and interpretation.
